APP NAME (max 30)
AddStock: Option Builder

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Build option strategies and see payoff, breakeven and Greeks before you trade.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
AddStock helps you plan options trades on Indian indices and stocks, on live NSE market data. Build a strategy leg by leg and see what it could make or lose before you place a single order.

STRATEGY BUILDER
• Add calls, puts and futures, buy or sell, on any strike and expiry
• See the payoff chart, max profit, max loss and breakeven points at once
• Net entry credit or debit worked out for you
• Delta, Gamma, Theta and Vega for every leg and for the whole strategy
• Start from ready-made templates such as straddles, strangles, spreads and iron condors

DRAFT PORTFOLIOS
• Save a strategy as a draft with its entry prices
• Track its P&L over time, exit one leg or all of them, and keep a record of closed trades

OPTION CHAIN
• Calls and puts side by side with LTP, open interest and change for every strike
• Add a strike to your strategy straight from the chain

OPEN INTEREST
• Open interest and change in OI by strike
• Highest call OI and highest put OI, to show where support and resistance may be building

CHARTS
• Candle and line charts
• Indicators, including moving averages and Bollinger Bands
• Drawing tools: trend lines, horizontal lines and Fibonacci retracement
• Compare one symbol with another, replay past bars, and set price alerts

DAILY MARKET REPORT
• A one-page summary of the market: index moves, FII / DII activity, global market sentiment, gold, the rupee against the US dollar, and key market news
• Ready to share

PORTFOLIO
• Record your stock and futures buys and sells for India and the US
• See your holdings, invested amount, current value, booked and open P&L, and allocation
• For US holdings, see your currency gain on the rupee as well

Light and dark themes are both included.

AddStock is an analysis tool. It is not investment advice and it does not place orders. Prices, margins and Greeks are estimates; check them with your broker before you trade. Trading options carries risk, and you can lose more than you expect.
