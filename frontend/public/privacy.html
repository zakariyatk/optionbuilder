AddStock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy covers AddStock: the web app at optionbuilder.adddots.com and the AddStock Android app (com.optionbuilder). Both are run by ADD DOTS ("we", "us"). It explains what information we collect, why we collect it and what you can do about it.

The Android app

The Android app doesn't currently collect personal information or send any to us. It has no sign-in and asks for no permissions other than internet access. Anything you enter stays on your device. If a later version adds sign-in or online features, we'll update this policy first.

Information we collect in the web app

  • Your Google account. You sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address and Google account ID, and use them only to check that your email is on the list of approved users and to keep you signed in. We never see your Google password.
  • What you save. The strategy drafts and portfolio transactions you save are stored on our server so you can open them again.
  • Upstox connection (optional). If you connect your Upstox account, Upstox gives us an access token. We use it only to fetch market data and margin figures you ask for. AddStock can't place orders, and we never see your Upstox password.
  • Technical data. Like any web server, ours records basic request details such as IP address, browser type and time of access. We use these only to keep the service running and secure.

Cookies

We set a single cookie that keeps you signed in. It expires when you sign out or after a few hours. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't track you across other sites.

How we use your information

We use it only to provide AddStock: signing you in, showing market data and keeping your saved work. We don't sell your information, use it for advertising or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Services we rely on

  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Supabase runs our sign-in service.
  • Upstox provides market data, plus your broker connection if you choose to connect.
  • Hostinger hosts our website and server.

Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy.

Storage and security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Saved drafts and portfolios are kept on our server in an area that can't be reached from the web. Anyone signed in to the same AddStock service can see them, so don't save anything you don't want other approved users to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details and saved work while you use AddStock. Server logs are kept for a short time and then deleted. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • Delete your drafts and portfolios in the app, or ask us to delete them.
  • Disconnect Upstox at any time from your Upstox account settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us at the address below.

Children

AddStock is a tool for the stock market and isn't meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Not financial advice

AddStock shows market data and calculations to help you analyse trades. None of it is investment advice.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

Contact

For any question or request about your information, email support@adddots.com.
